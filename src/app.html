Programorder is training order

Sets is the planned count. Low and high are the rep range the progression rule reads. Step is the load jump to take once the range is maxed out. Superset pairs an exercise with the one above it, and every session logged from then on starts with that pairing.

Band programseparate from the gym program above

A second, independent set of sessions for band-only training — travel, a rehab block, anywhere the gym isn't an option. Step is the band's rated kg; the progression note reads it as the next weight to stack once the rep range is maxed out. These sessions count toward the weekly session floor and target the same as the gym program, but their sets are not counted against the gym program's weekly volume targets, and vice versa.

Your data lives in a private GitHub repository. Every save commits there, so the repo is the record and this browser is only a cache. The token is stored on this device alone, kept out of every export, and never leaves your browser except to api.github.com.

Check-in exporttier 1

A condensed snapshot of the last 7 to 14 days: weight trend, volume against your program's own weekly targets, top lift changes, nutrition averages, and flags. Enough for a check-in review without the raw rows.

Full historytier 2

Every row, one CSV per table, bundled as a ZIP: weigh-ins, training sets, nutrition, InBody check-ins, cardio, mobility, rehab. Pull this when the question needs the raw series, like whether a lift has actually stalled over eight weeks.
